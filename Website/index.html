Expert Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026
PrivacySupport

Expert is a skill and practice-time tracking application developed by Nado Labs. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Expert.

Information stored by Expert

Expert lets you store skill names, practice-session dates and durations, optional notes, progress and milestone information, active or paused timer information, and app appearance preferences. This information is stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

Nado Labs does not collect, receive, or transmit the information you enter into Expert. The current version does not require an account, include advertising or third-party analytics, track users, sell personal information, or request access to contacts, photos, cameras, microphones, location, or health information.

How information is used

Information entered into Expert is used only on your device to provide the app’s features, including tracking practice sessions, calculating progress, displaying milestones, and presenting skill summaries.

Data sharing

Nado Labs does not share your Expert data with third parties because the app does not transmit that data to Nado Labs or external services. Apple may separately process information related to downloading, purchasing, updating, backing up, or using apps according to Apple’s terms and privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Expert retains your information locally until you delete it. You can delete individual skills and sessions, or remove all locally stored information under Settings → Your Data → Delete All App Data. Deleting Expert also removes its local application data, subject to device backups managed through your Apple account and device settings.

Because Nado Labs does not receive or maintain your Expert data, Nado Labs cannot access, recover, export, or delete it remotely.

Data security

Expert relies on the security features provided by iOS and your device to protect locally stored information. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device and Apple account security.

Children’s privacy

Expert is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users, and it does not transmit user-entered information to Nado Labs.

Changes to this policy

Nado Labs may update this policy if Expert’s features or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when changes are published. If a future version introduces accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, or third-party services, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions or assistance with Expert, visit the Nado Labs support page.

Expert is developed by Nado Labs.
