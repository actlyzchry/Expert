PrivacySupport

Before requesting support

Expert stores its learning records locally. Nado Labs cannot view or recover your skills, notes, or session history remotely. Please do not include private practice notes or other sensitive information in a support request.

Contact Nado Labs

Use the public support tracker to report a problem, request a feature, or ask a question about Expert.

Open a support request

Deleting your information

In Expert, open Settings → Your Data → Delete All App Data. You can also remove the app from your device to delete its local application data, subject to your Apple backup settings.
