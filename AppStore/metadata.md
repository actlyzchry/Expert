## App description

Mastery is built one focused session at a time.

Expert is a private practice tracker for any skill you want to improve. Create a skill, start the timer, and turn the time you invest into clear progress and meaningful milestones.

TRACK FOCUSED PRACTICE

Start, pause, resume, and save practice sessions. Expert keeps an active session available when you leave the timer, so you can return without losing your progress.

BUILD MOMENTUM

Early milestones begin at 15 minutes, then grow from one hour toward long-term mastery. Progress bars make short sessions feel visible while keeping the larger journey in view.

REVIEW YOUR HISTORY

See when you started learning, total practice time, recent sessions, average session duration, current level, and the date you last practiced.

MANAGE YOUR SKILLS

Rename, archive, restore, or delete skills. Add past sessions manually, include optional notes, and correct your history when needed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Expert does not require an account. Your skills, practice notes, sessions, and timer information remain locally on your device. The current version contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Whether you are learning an instrument, developing a craft, studying a subject, training for a sport, or building a professional skill, Expert helps you stay focused on the work that creates improvement.

## App Review notes

Expert is a local, account-free practice-time tracker. No sign-in or demo credentials are required.

Suggested review path:

1. Tap New Skill and enter a skill name.
2. Tap Continue to view the milestone screen.
3. Tap Start Tracking Time to begin a session.
4. Pause, resume, or stop and save the session.
5. Return to My Skills to view the saved skill and recent session.
6. Open Skill Summary to review totals and session metadata.
7. Open Settings to view appearance options, privacy information, data deletion, the public privacy policy, and support.

All user-entered skill and practice data is stored locally on the device. The app does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party data services. Reviewers can remove all test data under Settings → Your Data → Delete All App Data.
